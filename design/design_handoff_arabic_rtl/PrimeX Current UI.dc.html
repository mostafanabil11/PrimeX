What I measured

The page is ~7,800px tall on a 375px screen. Nine full-width bands, each with its own eyebrow, 36px display heading, red rule, body paragraph and link — the section header alone is ~190px before any content appears.

The 320px hero photo pushes the headline below the fold. Every card is full width and stacked, so a two-card "preview" reads as an index. Four accordion rows and a facilities grid of four full-width tiles repeat the same rhythm five times.

The 88px logo overhanging a 64px bar, plus the 40px strip and the marquee, spend ~150px of chrome before the page begins.

What breaks here

The total is at the bottom. The summary is a lg:sticky aside in a lg:grid-cols-[3fr_2fr], so on a phone it drops below the form. The price you are agreeing to is off-screen at the moment you tap Pay.

The stepper wraps to two or three lines at 375px, and its labels sit at the 12px caps floor — four steps, twelve rules, ~70px of chrome that never changes.

Step 2 asks one question. A whole screen, a heading, a stepper move and a Continue tap for a single date field.

Nothing reassures. No card marks, no "secured by Paymob" lockup, no cancellation line near the button — the one page where a stranger is handing over money.
